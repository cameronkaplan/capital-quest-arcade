## Borderline Hotline

Style: overexcited call-in radio show, quick and weird, 4-6 seconds each.

### `hotline-nh-vt.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Hi, this is New Hampshire. I touch Maine, but my look-alike neighbor is immediately west of me. Please stop calling us twins."

On-screen prompt:
"Borderline Hotline. Name that neighbor."

### `hotline-al-ms.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Alabama here. Georgia is on one side, but the state on my other side gets mixed up with me all the time."

On-screen prompt:
"Who is west of Alabama?"

### `hotline-ca-or-wa.mp3`
Caller voice:
"California wants to drive up the coast to Washington, but I am standing right in the middle."

On-screen prompt:
"Name the coastal middle state."

### `hotline-tx-nm-az.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Texas says it can almost reach Arizona, except I am politely blocking the way."

On-screen prompt:
"Who is between Texas and Arizona?"

### `hotline-dakota-twins.mp3`
Caller voice:
"North Dakota keeps calling me the lower twin. Rude, but accurate."

On-screen prompt:
"Which Dakota is below?"

## Capital Call-In

Style: one caller leaving a weird hotline message. No host voice, no back-and-forth dialog.

### `capital-boise-idaho.mp3`
Caller voice:
"This is Boise. People keep hearing boys-see, but I need the state I belong to."

On-screen prompt:
"Boise is calling from which state?"

### `capital-juneau-alaska.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Juneau the answer? I am cold, far northwest, and absolutely not Anchorage."

On-screen prompt:
"Juneau is the capital of which state?"

### `capital-sacramento-california.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Everyone yells Los Angeles or San Francisco. I am Sacramento, asking for basic respect."

On-screen prompt:
"Sacramento belongs to which state?"

### `capital-nashville-tennessee.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Music City calling. I have guitars, a state capital job, and zero patience for Memphis guesses."

On-screen prompt:
"Nashville is the capital of which state?"

### `capital-denver-colorado.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Mile-high caller here. I am Denver, and I need my mountain state."

On-screen prompt:
"Denver belongs to which state?"

### `capital-boston-massachusetts.mp3`
Caller voice:
"This is Boston. Please pahk me in the right New England state."

On-screen prompt:
"Boston is calling from which state?"

## Midwest Capital Hotline Additions

Use one voice only. The text versions are already in the game. After these MP3s exist in `assets/audio/`, they can be wired to the matching Capital Call-In cases.

### `hotline-i-state-capitals.mp3`
Caller voice:
"I am calling with an I-state emergency. Illinois got ill from noisy springs, so Illinois goes with Springfield. Indiana practically says Indianapolis if you keep talking long enough. Iowa owes ya coins, so Iowa gets Des Moines. Illinois-Springfield, Indiana-Indianapolis, Iowa-Des Moines. Please return all three I-states to their proper bins."

### `hotline-dakota-capitals.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Dakota capital twin report. North Dakota is the upper Dakota, and its capital is Bismarck, which sounds like a very serious history teacher. South Dakota is the lower Dakota, and its capital is Pierre, which sounds fancy but is absolutely in South Dakota. North-Bismarck, South-Pierre. The twins may now stop wearing the same outfit."

### `hotline-great-lakes-capitals.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Great Lakes lost and found calling. Michigan lands in Lansing. Wisconsin is mad about Madison. Minnesota brought mini sodas for Saint Paul. Mitten-Lansing, cheese-wedge-Madison, MIMAL-hat-Saint Paul. If Detroit tries to answer for Michigan, put it back on hold."

### `hotline-rectangle-capitals.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Rectangle capital office calling. Kansas went to peek at Dorothy's house, so Kansas gets Topeka. Nebraska filed its paperwork with Lincoln. North Dakota has Bismarck, South Dakota has Pierre. The flat states are not all the same rectangle. Some are rectangles with jobs."

### `hotline-ohio-missouri-capitals.mp3`
Caller voice:
"Ohio and Missouri mix-up desk. Ohio gets Columbus: oh, hi, Mr. Columbus. Missouri gets Jefferson City. St. Louis is famous, but Jefferson City is the capital. Ohio-Columbus, Missouri-Jefferson City. St. Louis has been asked to stop pretending."
